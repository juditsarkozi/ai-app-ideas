Questions

What is an AI app blueprint?

A blueprint is a complete plan for an app idea: who it is for, the problem it solves, the core features and how it could make money. It is written so you can paste it into an AI coding tool and start building.

How do I use a blueprint with Bolt, Lovable or Cursor?

Open the blueprint, copy it, and give it to your AI builder as the first prompt. The AI then builds the right screens and features from the start instead of guessing from a one-line idea.

Can I get a blueprint for my own app idea?
